# Business Solutions

Explore enterprise mobile-agent controls, field evidence, insurance, device operations, model evaluation and inventory assistance. Each topic distinguishes what is implemented, what is demonstrated in a simulator and what is only a proposed integration. This collection is **local, read-only product discovery**: it installs no enterprise backend, grants no device access and does not enable remote control.

The first candidate is an approval-gated trust layer plus a synthetic Fieldglass field-workbench flow. Insurance, fleet, model management and commerce require separate permissioned pilots and domain-level authorization.
