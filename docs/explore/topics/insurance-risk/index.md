# Insurance Risk Assistant

**Today:** iGentic has policy/draft primitives, and separate catastrophe-data and stochastic-insurance research exists. **There is no working iPhone-to-insurance-engine integration or approved risk model here.**

**B2B use case:** a fictional survey of an insured building produces a structured proposal. After human review, a future independently authorized domain adapter could request a **synthetic** risk-review result with explicit provenance and assumptions.

**Missing before a pilot:** permitted source data and licenses, independent model validation, tenant isolation, geolocation/privacy controls, actual backend authorization, failure receipts and human-underwriter review. Never display prototype output as a premium, coverage decision, Solvency II value or production catastrophe loss.
